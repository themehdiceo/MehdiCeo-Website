## 9. Content quality gates (publish checklist)

Before indexing any new or updated page:

- [ ] **Unique:** Does not repeat an existing cornerstone page's primary intent
- [ ] **Sourced:** Facts traceable to `profile-source-of-truth.md` or owner verification
- [ ] **No fabrication:** No invented stats, testimonials, or case studies
- [ ] **Disclaimer:** Financial/business claims include non-guarantee language
- [ ] **Branded:** Natural mention of Mehdi CEO and/or themehdiceo (not stuffed)
- [ ] **Internal links:** Minimum 3 contextual links to cornerstones
- [ ] **Metadata:** Title 50–60 chars, description 150–160 chars, unique
- [ ] **Schema:** Article schema only on blog posts; FAQ schema only on `/faq`
- [ ] **Arabic:** AR version ready or page stays FR-only with hreflang exclusion until ready
- [ ] **Abandoned names:** No "Mehdi West" or deprecated branding

## 11. What we are explicitly NOT doing

- **No new money claims** beyond owner-verified personal history
